At Digital Training Academy, we are committed to empowering individuals with the skills they need to thrive in today's digital landscape...

32,000 Nigerians to Be Empowered with Free World-Class Training

The Federal Government of Nigeria, in partnership with Coursera, is offering 32,000 free training courses to empower citizens with in-demand digital skills.

This initiative is part of the National Skilling Project, designed to bridge the skills gap, enhance employability, and create new opportunities for Nigerians in today's competitive job market.

The government launched this program to ensure every Nigerian, regardless of background or resources, can participate in the digital economy.

Beyond providing free online training, training centers have been set up across the country. This means participants can learn at centers or with their own personal gadgets to fully participate and benefit from the program.
